## Full description
Mehfil is the operations app for Indian wedding-service vendors — decorators, caterers, photographers, light-and-sound crews. It replaces the spreadsheet, the WhatsApp scroll and the diary with one calm screen.

The calendar is the spine. Every event sits on the month, the day and the season, and the moment two events overlap or a crew member is booked twice, Mehfil says so — before it becomes a problem on the morning.

Money is never hidden. The home screen shows what is outstanding across the season, what is overdue and who owes it. Each event has its payment schedule; recording a payment takes three taps on a large keypad, and reminders come pre-written in three tones for WhatsApp or SMS.

Crew and inventory, on any date. See who is available on a given Saturday, assign teams, take attendance on the morning with one tap per person, and know exactly how many chairs, drapes and rigs are committed on any day of the season.

A page for the client. Share one clean link with what you are bringing, the day's timings and what has been received — no screenshots of spreadsheets.

Also: enquiries that convert to events with a conflict check, package templates for three-tap quotes, change requests that show their real cost, expenses with the margin, and a season summary against your target.

Sign in with Google or Apple. Your data is stored securely in your own account, works offline, and can be erased or deleted from inside the app at any time.
